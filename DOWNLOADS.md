## Versions not yet available

Not every Minecraft release or loader has a verified precompiled JAR in this archive. In particular, **NeoForge is not represented by a verified JAR in the existing files**. For newer releases, use the [source build workflow](.github/workflows/build-upstream.yml), then upload successful, tested JARs before publishing direct download links. For historical missing versions, first locate matching upstream source commits. Do not rename a JAR to claim compatibility with a different Minecraft version.
