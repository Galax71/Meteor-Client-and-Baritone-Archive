## Important distinctions

- Meteor Client's earliest historical JARs in the reviewed community archive target Minecraft 1.14.4; older Minecraft releases are not automatically 'lost Meteor versions'.
- Baritone's documented mapping begins with Minecraft 1.12; earlier support is not established.
- A Baritone major-version mapping does not confirm every patch version or mod loader.
- `1.16.x` and combined version folders are not counted as exact-version evidence.
